Privacy Policy

Piggy Bank – Kids Savings · Effective November 4, 2026

Piggy Bank is made by Vibha Mane (Keep it Simple Apps).

We don't collect any data

Piggy Bank has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Everything stays on your iPhone

The names, amounts and notes you enter are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They're never sent to us or to anyone else.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Piggy Bank's data may be included in that backup, which you and Apple control.

Deleting the app

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Piggy Bank is meant to be used by parents together with their kids. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
