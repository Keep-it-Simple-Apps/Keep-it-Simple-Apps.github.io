Piggy Bank – Kids Savings

Does Piggy Bank move real money?

No. It's a pretend piggy that remembers the money you've promised. When your kids cash out, you pay them or buy their treat for real.

I added money by mistake. How do I fix it?

After a quick add (dragging the $1 coin), tap Undo on the new history row within a few seconds. For anything else, record the opposite: a cash-out to remove extra money, or a deposit to put money back.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

I got a new iPhone. Will my piggy banks move over?

Yes, if you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

Can I turn off the sounds?

Tap the speaker button at the top of a piggy bank screen. Sounds also follow your iPhone's silent switch.
