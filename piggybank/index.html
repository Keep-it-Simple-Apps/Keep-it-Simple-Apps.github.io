Piggy Bank – Kids Savings

Track the coins your kids earn.

Oink! Meet the piggy bank that never gets lost under the bed.

Piggy Bank keeps track of the money you've promised your kids, without a jar of coins or a bank account. Each child gets their own piggy in their favorite color. When they earn something, drop in a coin and watch it fall into the slot with a happy jingle!

  • A piggy for every kid: each child has their own balance and history.
  • Earn it, drop it in: pick coins or type an amount, and add a note so everyone remembers why.
  • Quick add: drag the $1 coin straight into the piggy.
  • Shake out the savings: cash out for a treat and watch the coins tumble out.
  • History: every deposit and cash-out, with notes, grouped by month.

Piggy Bank doesn't hold or move real money. When your kids cash out, you pay them or buy their treat for real.
